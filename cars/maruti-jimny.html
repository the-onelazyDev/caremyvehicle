📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Maruti Suzuki Jimny 5-Door is a purebred off-roader designed to conquer any terrain. Built on a rigid ladder frame chassis with 3-link rigid axle coil-spring suspension, ALLGRIP PRO 4WD system with low-range transfer gear (4L), 210mm ground clearance, and standard 6 airbags across all variants, it brings authentic 4x4 heritage with everyday usability.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.5L K15B 4-Cylinder Petrol Engine (104.8 PS / 134.2 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 3985 mm (L) × 1645 mm (W) × 1720 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2590 mm)
