📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The all-new second-generation Škoda Kodiaq brings Czech flagship luxury to India with a 204 PS / 320 Nm 2.0L TSI EVO engine, intelligent 4x4 all-wheel drive with 7-speed DSG, crystalline illuminated front grille, Dark Chrome D-pillar, Škoda Smart Dials with haptic rotary screens, a massive 32.77 cm (12.9-inch) freestanding infotainment display, 13-speaker 725W Canton sound system, Ergo pneumatic massaging front seats, and 9 airbags standard.

Engine Options & Performance: 2.0L TSI EVO Turbo Petrol (204 PS / 150 kW @ 4500-6000 rpm, 320 Nm @ 1500-4400 rpm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4758 mm L × 1864 mm W × 1659 mm H (Wheelbase: 2791 mm)
