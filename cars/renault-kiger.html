📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

Updated for MY26 with 6 airbags standard across the range, Renault's new diamond emblem, semi-leatherette ventilated seats/upholstery, auto headlamps, and rain-sensing wipers, the Renault Kiger offers segment-leading 405-litre boot space, 205 mm ground clearance, Multi-Sense drive modes (Eco, Normal, Sport), 3D Sound by Arkamys (8 speakers), wireless Android Auto & Apple CarPlay, and a choice of 72 PS 1.0L Energy and 100 PS 1.0L Turbo petrol engines.

Engine Options & Performance: 999 cc 1.0L Energy 3-Cylinder Petrol (72 PS @ 6250 rpm, 96 Nm @ 3500 rpm) – 5MT / 5-Speed Easy-R AMT,999 cc 1.0L Turbo 3-Cylinder Petrol (100 PS @ 5000 rpm, 160 Nm MT / 152 Nm CVT) – 5MT / X-Tronic CVT

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 3991 mm L x 1750 mm W x 1605 mm H (Wheelbase: 2500 mm)
