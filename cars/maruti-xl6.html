📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Maruti Suzuki XL6 is NEXA’s premium 6-seater MPV engineered for executive family travel. Featuring individual plush 2nd-row captain seats with one-touch recline and individual armrests, ventilated front seats, 360-degree HD view camera, Tyre Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS), 6-speed automatic with paddle shifters, and certified 26.32 km/kg S-CNG efficiency.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.5L K15C DualJet Dual VVT Smart Hybrid Petrol (103.06 PS / 136.8 Nm) | 1.5L S-CNG (87.83 PS / 121.5 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4445 mm (L) × 1775 mm (W) × 1755 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2740 mm)
