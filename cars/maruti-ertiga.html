📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Maruti Suzuki Ertiga is India’s undisputed bestselling 7-seater MPV, trusted by over a million families. Powered by the refined 1.5L K15C Smart Hybrid Dual VVT engine (103 PS / 136.8 Nm) paired with either a 5-speed manual or a 6-speed automatic with paddle shifters, achieving 20.51 km/l (MT) and 20.30 km/l (AT). Also available with factory S-CNG delivering class-leading 26.11 km/kg economy. Features 7-inch SmartPlay Pro touchscreen, second-row roof-mounted AC with independent blower controls, air-cooled cup holders, and versatile one-touch recline and slide seats.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.5L K15C Smart Hybrid (103 PS / 136.8 Nm) | 1.5L S-CNG (87.8 PS / 121.5 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4395 mm (L) × 1735 mm (W) × 1690 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2740 mm)
