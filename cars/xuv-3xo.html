📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Mahindra XUV 3XO thoroughly disrupts India’s sub-4 metre compact SUV category by democratizing luxury features previously reserved for vehicles twice its price. It introduces the segment’s first and largest Panoramic Skyroof, full Level 2 ADAS (featuring radar and camera systems with 10 proactive safety features like adaptive cruise control with stop-and-go, lane keep assist, and autonomous emergency braking), 360-degree surround-view camera with blind view monitor, and segment-exclusive dual-zone automatic climate control. Engine prowess is class-leading: 1.2L mStallion TGDi Turbo Petrol churning out 130 PS and 230 Nm (fastest in segment: 0-60 km/h in 4.5s), 1.2L TCMPFi Turbo Petrol (111 PS / 200 Nm), and 1.5L CRDe Turbo Diesel (117 PS / 300 Nm). Transmission choices include a smooth 6-speed manual and a world-renowned AISIN 6-speed torque converter automatic.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.2L TCMPFi Turbo (111 PS) | 1.2L mStallion TGDi Turbo (130 PS) | 1.5L CRDe Turbo Diesel (117 PS)
