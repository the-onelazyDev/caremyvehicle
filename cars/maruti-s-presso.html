📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Maruti Suzuki S-Presso is India’s signature mini-SUV that punches above its weight with a bold upright stance, commanding high seating position, and generous 180mm ground clearance. Powered by the efficient 1.0L Next-Gen K-Series Dual Jet Dual VVT engine (67 PS / 89 Nm) with Idle Start-Stop delivering up to 25.30 km/l on AGS automatic and 32.73 km/kg on factory S-CNG. Features a unique circular center console with 7-inch SmartPlay Studio touchscreen with smartphone navigation, dynamic digital speedometer, and high approach and departure angles.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.0L K10C Dual Jet Dual VVT (67 PS / 89 Nm) | 1.0L S-CNG (56.7 PS / 82.1 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 3565 mm (L) × 1520 mm (W) × 1567 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2380 mm)
