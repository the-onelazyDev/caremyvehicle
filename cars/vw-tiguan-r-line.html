📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

Imported as a full German CBU on the MQB evo platform, the all-new Volkswagen Tiguan R-Line delivers 204 PS & 320 Nm from its 2.0L TSI EVO engine paired with a 7-speed DSG and 4MOTION all-wheel drive, Dynamic Chassis Control (DCC Pro) with two-valve adaptive dampers, R-Line sport seats with pneumatic massage, a massive 38.1 cm (15-inch) infotainment screen, Head-Up Display, 19-inch Coventry diamond-turned alloys, and 9 airbags with 21 Level-2 ADAS features.

Engine Options & Performance: 2.0L TSI EVO Turbo Petrol (204 PS @ 4500-6000 rpm, 320 Nm @ 1500-4400 rpm)
