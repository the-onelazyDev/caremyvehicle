📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Toyota Hilux is the world's benchmark for extreme-durability lifestyle utility. Built on the heavy-duty IMV platform with a rigid ladder-frame chassis, it pairs a potent 2.8L D-4D turbo diesel producing 201 bhp and 500 Nm of torque with electronic 4x4, rear diff-lock, active traction control (A-TRC), 700 mm water wading capability, and a versatile double-cab cargo deck.

Engine Options & Performance: 2.8L 4-Cylinder D-4D Turbo Diesel (201 bhp / 500 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 5325 mm (L) × 1855 mm (W) × 1815 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 3085 mm)
