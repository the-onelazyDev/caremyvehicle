📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Kia EV6 is a flagship high-performance electric crossover built on the dedicated Electric-Global Modular Platform (E-GMP) with 800V ultra-fast architecture. Featuring an upgraded 84 kWh high-density battery providing 663 km certified range, dual motors producing 325 PS and 605 Nm torque with AWD delivering 0-100 km/h in 5.3s, 10% to 80% charging in just 18 minutes via 350 kW DC charger, dual 12.3-inch curved panoramic display cockpit, 14-speaker Meridian audio, premium relaxation front seats with zero-gravity recline, and Level 2 ADAS 2.0 with 25 safety functions.

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4695 mm (L) × 1890 mm (W) × 1570 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2900 mm (Long EV Architecture Wheelbase with Flat Floor Cabin))
