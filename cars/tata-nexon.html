📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Tata Nexon is India’s undisputed bestselling compact SUV, celebrated for its iconic futuristic design, 5-Star Bharat NCAP safety rating with 6 airbags standard, class-leading 208 mm ground clearance, dual 10.25-inch high-definition digital screens with wireless smartphone mirroring, 9-speaker JBL acoustic sound system with subwoofer, voice-assisted panoramic-style electric sunroof, connected X-Factor full-width LED taillamps, and advanced powertrain choices including 1.2L Turbo Petrol with quick-shifting 7-speed DCA and 1.5L Revotorq Diesel.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.2L Turbocharged Revotron Petrol (120 PS / 170 Nm) | 1.5L Turbocharged Revotorq Diesel (115 PS / 260 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 3995 mm (L) × 1804 mm (W) × 1620 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2498 mm)
