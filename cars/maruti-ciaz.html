📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Maruti Suzuki Ciaz is NEXA’s flagship executive sedan. Featuring a generous 2650mm wheelbase delivering best-in-class rear lounge legroom, cavernous 510-litre boot, 1.5L K15B Smart Hybrid petrol engine with dual-battery system, plush leather upholstery, and sharp LED projector headlamps with DRLs, it is tailored for premium chauffeur-driven and self-driven comfort alike.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.5L K15B Smart Hybrid Petrol (104.7 PS / 138 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4490 mm (L) × 1730 mm (W) × 1485 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2650 mm (Best-in-Class Rear Legroom))
