📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

Built on the German MQB-A0-IN platform with a 5-Star Global NCAP safety rating for both adult and child occupants, the new Volkswagen Taigun features an illuminated front & rear VW crest with infinite LED tail lamps, a panoramic sunroof, an all-new 8-speed automatic transmission on the 1.0L TSI, the exhilarating 150 PS 1.5L TSI EVO with Active Cylinder Management (ACT) & 7-speed DSG, and 6-way electrically adjustable ventilated front seats with subwoofer audio.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.0L TSI Turbo Petrol (115 PS @ 5000-5500 rpm, 178 Nm @ 1750-4500 rpm),1.5L TSI EVO Turbo Petrol with Active Cylinder Management (150 PS @ 5000-6000 rpm, 250 Nm @ 1600-3500 rpm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4221 mm L × 1760 mm W × 1612 mm H (Wheelbase: 2651 mm)
