📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The All-New Celerio is India’s most fuel-efficient petrol hatchback, delivering up to 26.68 km/l with the Next-Gen 1.0L K10C Dual Jet engine with idle start-stop, and an astonishing 34.43 km/kg on factory S-CNG. It features 3D organic sculpted styling, 7-inch SmartPlay Studio touchscreen, keyless entry with push start, 313L large boot, and 15-inch black alloys.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.0L K10C Next-Gen Dual Jet Dual VVT (67 PS / 89 Nm) | S-CNG (56.7 PS / 82.1 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 3695 mm (L) × 1655 mm (W) × 1555 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2435 mm)
