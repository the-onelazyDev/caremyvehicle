📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Tata Curvv EV spearheads India’s electric design renaissance as an aerodynamic luxury SUV Coupe built on Tata’s Acti.ev pure electric platform. Certified with a 5-Star Bharat NCAP crash safety score, the Curvv EV is offered in two liquid-cooled IP67 high-energy battery options: 45 kWh delivering 502 km certified range and a flagship 55 kWh pack delivering an astonishing 585 km single-charge driving range. Equipped with a permanent magnet synchronous motor generating up to 167 PS (123 kW) and instantaneous 215 Nm torque, propelling it from 0 to 100 km/h in just 8.6 seconds. Ultra-fast 70 kW DC charging replenishes 10% to 80% battery in 40 minutes, adding 150 km of driving range in 15 minutes. Advanced V2V (Vehicle-to-Vehicle) and V2L (Vehicle-to-Load 3.3 kW) charging technology transforms the vehicle into a mobile power bank.

Engine Options & Performance: Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor (150 PS on 45 kWh / 167 PS on 55 kWh) | 215 Nm Instant Torque

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4,310 mm (L) × 1,810 mm (W) × 1,637 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2,560 mm)
