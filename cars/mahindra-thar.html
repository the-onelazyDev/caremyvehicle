📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Mahindra Thar 3-Door is India’s definitive off-road cultural icon, engineering uncompromised go-anywhere capability with modern everyday drivability. Built on a heavy-duty ladder-frame chassis with roll-over mitigation and awarded a proven 4-Star Global NCAP safety rating, the Thar is powered by robust powertrains: a 2.0L mStallion TGDi Turbo Petrol (150 PS / 320 Nm) and a 2.2L mHawk Turbo Diesel (130 PS / 300 Nm), alongside an ultra-accessible 1.5L D117 CRDe Diesel (117 PS / 300 Nm) in Rear-Wheel Drive (RWD) configuration. Transmission choices include a 6-speed manual and a 6-speed AISIN torque converter automatic. Equipped with true 4x4 hardware—including a mechanical shift-on-the-fly transfer case with low range (4L & 4H), mechanical locking rear differential (MLD), brake locking differentials, 41.8° approach and 36.8° departure angles, 226 mm ground clearance, and an unyielding 650 mm water wading capability. Inside, the wash-friendly adventure cabin features IP54 water-resistant switches, removable doors, 17.78 cm touchscreen with adventure telemetry gauges, roof-mounted acoustic speakers, and both Hardtop and Convertible open-air choices.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.5L D117 CRDe Diesel (117 PS / 300 Nm) | 2.0L mStallion Turbo Petrol (150 PS / 320 Nm) | 2.2L mHawk Diesel (130 PS / 300 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 3985 mm (L) × 1820 mm (W) × 1920 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2450 mm)
