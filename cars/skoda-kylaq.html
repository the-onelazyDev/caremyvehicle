📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

Built on the MQB-A0-IN platform, the Škoda Kylaq brings authentic European driving dynamics to the sub-4m SUV segment with a punchy 115 PS 1.0L TSI engine, segment-best 446-litre boot space, 5-star Bharat NCAP safety with 25+ standard safety features including 6 airbags, and segment-first 6-way electrically adjustable ventilated front seats.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.0L TSI Turbo Petrol (115 PS @ 5000-5500 rpm, 178 Nm @ 1750-4000 rpm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 3995 mm L × 1783 mm W × 1619 mm H (Wheelbase: 2566 mm)
