📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Mahindra Scorpio-N, celebrated as the "Big Daddy of SUVs", redefines the authentic D-segment body-on-frame SUV experience in India. Engineered on a third-generation stiffened steel chassis with Frequency Selective Damping (FSD) and Penta-link rear suspension, it is powered by class-leading engines: a 2.0L mStallion Turbo Petrol delivering 200 PS and 380 Nm, or a 2.2L mHawk Turbo Diesel producing up to 175 PS and 400 Nm, mated to 6-speed manual or AISIN 6-speed automatic transmissions. Equipped with the legendary 4XPLOR intelligent terrain response system (Normal, Grass/Gravel/Snow, Mud/Ruts, Sand) with mechanical rear locking differential, an Adrenox-powered 20.32 cm touchscreen with built-in Alexa, a 12-speaker Sony 3D spatial sound system, dual-zone climate control, and 5-Star Global NCAP crash safety.

Engine Options & Performance: 2.0L mStallion Turbo Petrol (200 PS / 380 Nm) | 2.2L mHawk Turbo Diesel (132/175 PS, up to 400 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4662 mm (L) × 1917 mm (W) × 1857 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2750 mm)
