📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Maruti Suzuki Invicto is NEXA’s ultra-premium flagship hybrid UV. Powered by a potent 2.0L Intelligent Electric Hybrid powertrain producing 186 PS with e-CVT delivering an impressive 23.24 km/l fuel economy, it offers first-class luxury with powered Ottoman captain seats, a panoramic sliding sunroof with mood ambient lighting, 10.1-inch SmartPlay Magnum touchscreen, powered tailgate, and 6 airbags standard.

Engine Options & Performance: 2.0L Intelligent Electric Strong Hybrid Atkinson Cycle Petrol (186 PS combined output)
