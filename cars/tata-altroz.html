Tata Altroz

The Tata Altroz is India’s benchmark "Gold Standard" premium hatchback, built on the advanced ALFA-ARC (Agile Light Flexible Advanced Architecture) and awarded an exemplary 5-Star Adult Safety Rating by Global NCAP. It offers India's most versatile powertrain spectrum: an efficient 1.2L Revotron 3-cylinder Petrol (88 PS / 115 Nm), an innovative Twin-Cylinder iCNG (73.5 PS / 103 Nm) that cleverly tucks dual cylinders beneath the luggage floor to preserve a class-leading 210 litres of usable boot space, a high-torque 1.5L Revotorq 4-cylinder Turbo Diesel (90 PS / 200 Nm) delivering up to 23.64 kmpl, and a performance-tuned 1.2L i-Turbo+ Petrol (120 PS / 170 Nm) in the Altroz Racer. Transmission options feature a slick 5-speed manual and a world-class Wet-Clutch 7-Speed DCA (Dual-Clutch Automatic) with machine learning shift logic. The cabin boasts 90-degree wide-opening doors, a 26.03 cm (10.25") HD touchscreen by HARMAN, 17.78 cm digital cluster, voice-assisted electric sunroof, 360-degree surround camera with blind-view monitor, wireless charging, and plush leatherette ventilated front seats.
