📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Toyota Fortuner is India's reigning full-size 7-seater SUV, celebrated for legendary reliability, heavy-duty body-on-frame chassis, and formidable 4x4 off-road capabilities. Powered by a brawny 2.8L D-4D diesel generating 201 bhp and an immense 500 Nm of torque, it features an electronic drive control (High/Low range), auto limited-slip differential (LSD), ventilated leather seats, 11-speaker JBL audio, and active traction control.

Engine Options & Performance: 2.8L 4-Cyl D-4D Turbo Diesel (201 bhp / 500 Nm) | 2.7L Petrol (164 bhp / 245 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4795 mm (L) × 1855 mm (W) × 1835 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2745 mm)
