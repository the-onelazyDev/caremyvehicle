# 🚀 Care My Vehicle: New Car Launches Playbook
> **Phase 1 Startup Focus:** Be the fastest, most granular, and most transparent source for **Every New Car Launch in India** (New Models, Facelifts, Special Editions, and Variant Updates).

---

## 🎯 Why This Launch-First Strategy is a Winning Move
Trying to index 15 years of old cars right away slows down a startup. By laser-focusing on **New Launches**:
1. **Search & Social Spikes:** Every new launch generates 10x-50x search volume spikes on Google & Instagram.
2. **Beat Legacy Media on Speed:** CarDekho & CarWale often take days to cleanly map all variant differences without clutter. If you post a clean, variant-by-variant breakdown within **30 minutes of launch**, you win instant virality.
3. **Highest Buyer Intent:** People searching for newly launched cars are ready with chequebooks to book.

## 📱 Instagram Content Templates for New Launches

### 1. Carousel Format (Highest Saves & Shares)
- **Slide 1 (Cover):** Striking shot of the new car + bold text:  
  *"`[Car Name]` Launched at ₹X.XX Lakh! All Variants & Prices Explained 📊"*
- **Slide 2:** Full variant price list table (Ex-showroom & on-road).
- **Slide 3:** Engine, power, torque & mileage cheat sheet.
- **Slide 4:** Variant comparison (Base vs Mid vs Top).
- **Slide 5:** The ONE variant you should actually book (VFM Pick).
- **Slide 6 (Call to Action):**  
  *"Save this post for your car hunt! Comment `[CAR NAME]` and we'll DM you the complete variant brochure."*

### 2. Reel Format (Highest Reach & Virality - 30 to 45 Seconds)
- **Hook (0-3s):**  
  *"Stop! Before you book the newly launched `[Car Name]`, watch this 30-second variant breakdown."*
- **Body (4-25s):**  
  *"The company launched X variants, but 90% of buyers should skip the top variant. Here’s why: Variant `[Mid]` saves you ₹2 Lakhs and only misses out on `[Minor Feature]`. Here is the exact on-road price breakdown..."*
- **CTA (26-30s):**  
  *"Follow @caremyvehicle for honest, zero-spam car launch details!"*

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Workflow for Each Launch Day

1. **At Event / Press Release (T-0 to T+15 Mins):**
   - Grab the official brochure PDF and ex-showroom price sheet.
2. **Drafting (T+15 to T+30 Mins):**
   - Fill the **Golden Specification Template**.
   - Calculate estimated on-road prices for top 5 cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai).
3. **Publishing (T+30 Mins):**
   - Publish live post on Care My Vehicle web/app.
   - Post Instagram Launch Reel & 6-slide Carousel.
4. **Follow-Up (Next 48 Hours):**
   - Post "Variant Battle" (New Launch vs #1 Competitor).
   - Post "Real Waiting Period & Delivery Updates".
