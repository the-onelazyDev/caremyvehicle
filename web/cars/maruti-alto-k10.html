📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The iconic Alto K10 now comes with standard 6 airbags, Electronic Stability Program (ESP), 7-inch SmartPlay Studio touchscreen with Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, and an ultra-frugal 1.0L Next-Gen K-Series Dual Jet engine delivering up to 33.40 km/kg on CNG.
