📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Maruti Suzuki Fronx is Nexa’s trendsetting compact coupe-SUV that combines athletic, aerodynamic fastback proportions with SUV toughness. Powered by either the punchy 1.0L K-Series Turbo Boosterjet engine (100.06 PS / 147.6 Nm with Progressive Smart Hybrid and paddle shifters) or the refined 1.2L Dual Jet engine (89.73 PS) available with manual, 5-speed AGS, and factory S-CNG (28.51 km/kg). Highlights include a 9-inch SmartPlay Pro+ touchscreen with wireless Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, Head-Up Display (HUD), 360-degree HD view camera, and 190mm ground clearance.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.0L K-Series Turbo Boosterjet (100.06 PS / 147.6 Nm) | 1.2L Dual Jet Dual VVT (89.73 PS / 113 Nm) | 1.2L S-CNG (77.5 PS / 98.5 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 3995 mm (L) × 1765 mm (W) × 1550 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2520 mm)
