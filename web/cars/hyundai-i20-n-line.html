📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Hyundai i20 N Line brings authentic World Rally Championship (WRC) motorsport excitement to India’s premium hatchback category. Powered by an enthusiastic 1.0L Kappa Turbo GDi petrol engine churning out 120 PS and 172 Nm of peak torque, it offers drivers the choice of an engaging 6-speed manual transmission or a lightning-fast 7-speed Dual Clutch Transmission (7DCT) with steering-mounted paddle shifters. Engineered for purists, the i20 N Line features a motorsport-tuned suspension with 30% stiffer damping for flat, responsive cornering, calibrated heavy-feel steering for sharp directional changes, all-4 disc brakes with striking red front calipers, and a signature twin-tip sports exhaust that barks with a throaty exhaust note. The cockpit is drenched in performance aesthetics with chequered-flag leatherette upholstery, red contrast stitching, red ambient mood lighting, sporty brushed metal pedals, and bespoke 3-spoke N steering wheel and N-embossed gear lever. High-end convenience is fully served by a 26.03 cm (10.25") HD infotainment screen with Apple CarPlay & Android Auto, Bose Premium 7-speaker sound system with subwoofer, voice-enabled smart electric sunroof, wireless charging pad, Bluelink connectivity, and standard 6 airbags.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.0L Kappa Turbo GDi Petrol (3 Cylinders, 998 cc, 120 PS @ 6000 rpm / 172 Nm @ 1500–4000 rpm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 3995 mm (L) × 1775 mm (W) × 1505 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2580 mm (Best-in-Class Cabin Space))
