📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The internationally acclaimed World Car of the Year, Hyundai IONIQ 5 is built on the revolutionary Electric Global Modular Platform (E-GMP) featuring an ultra-fast 800V charging architecture (10% to 80% charge in just 18 minutes on a 350 kW DC charger). Powered by a permanent magnet synchronous motor delivering rear-wheel-drive dynamics with up to 228.5 PS / 350 Nm and an ARAI-certified range of up to 631 km. The cabin redefines automotive interiors as a customizable Smart Living Space with eco-processed leather, a movable Universal Island sliding console, front relaxation seats with calf support, fixed glass Vision Roof, Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) bidirectional power supply up to 3.6 kW, and Hyundai SmartSense Level 2 ADAS with 21 advanced driver assistance features.

Engine Options & Performance: Rear-Mounted Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor (PMSM) (228.5 PS / 350 Nm) | 84 kWh Liquid-Cooled Lithium-Ion Battery (631 km ARAI Range)
