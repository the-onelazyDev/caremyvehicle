📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Maruti Suzuki Eeco is India’s undisputed king of practical family travel and versatile multi-purpose mobility. Powered by the refined 1.2L Advanced K-Series Dual Jet Dual VVT engine (80.76 PS / 104.4 Nm in petrol, 71.65 PS in CNG), the Eeco delivers an impressive 19.71 km/l petrol and 26.78 km/kg on factory S-CNG. Available in spacious 5-seater and 7-seater layouts with powerful air conditioning, digital instrument cluster, flat cabin floor, and huge 540L luggage capacity.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.2L Advanced K-Series Dual Jet Dual VVT (80.76 PS / 104.4 Nm) | S-CNG (71.65 PS / 95 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 3675 mm (L) × 1475 mm (W) × 1825 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2350 mm)
