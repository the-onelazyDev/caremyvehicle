📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Kia Carens redefines three-row family mobility as a progressive Recreational Vehicle (RV), merging the boldness of an SUV with the spacious versatility of an MPV. Standard with the Robust 15 Hi-Safety Package across every single variant—including 6 Airbags, All-Wheel Disc Brakes, ESC, VSM, HAC, Downhill Brake Control (DBC), and Highline TPMS. Power comes from four refined powertrain options: an energetic 1.5L Turbo GDi petrol (160 PS / 253 Nm) with 7DCT or 6iMT, a dependable 1.5L Smartstream naturally aspirated petrol (115 PS / 144 Nm) with 6MT, a punchy 1.5L CRDi VGT Diesel (116 PS / 250 Nm) with 6MT, 6iMT or 6-speed torque converter automatic, and a factory-fitted Bi-Fuel 1.5L CNG option delivering exceptional fuel efficiency. The lavish cabin offers 6-seater (Captain Seats) or 7-seater configurations, world-first One-Touch Easy Electric 2nd-Row Tumble, roof-flushed diffused AC vents for all three rows, 10.25-inch HD Touchscreen with Kia Connect, Bose 8-Speaker Audio, ventilated front seats, and SkyLight electric sunroof.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.5L Smartstream T-GDi Turbo Petrol (160 PS / 253 Nm) | 1.5L Smartstream MPi Petrol (115 PS / 144 Nm) | 1.5L CRDi VGT Diesel (116 PS / 250 Nm) | 1.5L Bi-Fuel CNG

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4540 mm (L) × 1800 mm (W) × 1708 mm (H) (with Roof Rails) (Wheelbase: 2780 mm (Longest Wheelbase in Segment with Maximum 3-Row Legroom))
