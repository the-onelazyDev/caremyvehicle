📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Tata Tiago is India’s benchmark entry-level family hatchback, renowned for setting the gold standard in cabin build quality and passenger protection with a certified 4-Star Global NCAP safety rating. Powered by the energetic 1.2L Revotron 3-cylinder Petrol engine producing 86 PS and 113 Nm of torque, it pairs with either a crisp 5-speed manual or a convenient 5-speed smart AMT automatic. For eco-conscious commuters, the groundbreaking Tiago iCNG introduces Tata's patented Twin-Cylinder technology—cleverly mounting dual cylinders under the boot floor to provide genuine usable luggage capacity alongside direct start in CNG mode and 26.49 km/kg efficiency. The modern dual-tone cabin includes a 17.78 cm touchscreen infotainment system by HARMAN with 8-speaker surround acoustics, digital instrument cluster, automatic climate control, rear parking camera, projector headlamps with LED DRLs, and corner stability control.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.2L Revotron 3-Cylinder Petrol (86 PS / 113 Nm) | 1.2L Bi-Fuel iCNG (73.5 PS / 95 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 3765 mm (L) × 1677 mm (W) × 1535 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2400 mm)
