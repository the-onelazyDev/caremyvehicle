📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Tata Punch is India’s undisputed #1 highest-selling passenger vehicle, pioneering the micro-SUV segment with authentic rugged SUV stance, class-leading 187 mm ground clearance, 5-Star Global & Bharat NCAP crash safety on the ALFA-ARC architecture, voice-assisted electric sunroof, 10.25-inch high-definition floating infotainment screen with wireless Android Auto & Apple CarPlay, 17.78 cm digital driver display, 8-speaker Harman acoustic audio, wireless charging, and responsive 1.2L Revotron petrol engine available with 5-speed manual, smart AMT with Traction Pro, or industry-leading twin-cylinder iCNG technology.
