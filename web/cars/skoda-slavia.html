❓ Frequently Asked Questions About Škoda Slavia

The ex-showroom price of the Škoda Slavia starts from ₹9.99 - ₹18.69 Lakh. On-road prices in Delhi NCR start from approximately ₹11.20 Lakh inclusive of state RTO registration, comprehensive insurance, and standard dealer logistics.
Under real-world Indian driving conditions, the Škoda Slavia returns approximately 12.5 to 14.5 kmpl in city traffic and 17 to 19.5 kmpl on highway expressways.
The Škoda Slavia comes equipped with 5.0 ★ (Global NCAP 5-Star Adult & 5-Star Child Safety), incorporating 6 airbags as standard, Electronic Stability Program (ESP), hill-hold assist, and ISOFIX child seat tethers.
The current waiting period stands at 2 - 5 Weeks depending on the chosen variant, powertrain, and exterior color combination.
The mid-level trims represent the sweet spot in the Škoda Slavia lineup, bundling essential safety tech, modern touchscreen connectivity, and alloy wheels at a competitive price point.
The Škoda Slavia comes backed by a comprehensive manufacturer standard warranty of 3 Years / 1,00,000 km, extendable up to 5 Years / 1,50,000 km with 24/7 Roadside Assistance.
