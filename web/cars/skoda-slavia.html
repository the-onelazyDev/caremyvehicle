📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The new Škoda Slavia combines timeless European coupé-inspired sedan elegance with a 5-Star Global NCAP safety rating, class-leading 179 mm ground clearance and 521-litre boot space, 360° area-view camera, sequential LED tail lamps, ventilated electric front seats, rear-seat massagers, an 8-speed torque converter AT on the 1.0 TSI, and the 150 PS 1.5 TSI EVO with 7-speed DSG.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.0L TSI Turbo Petrol (115 PS @ 5000-5500 rpm, 178 Nm @ 1750-4500 rpm),1.5L TSI EVO Turbo Petrol with Active Cylinder Technology (150 PS @ 5000-6000 rpm, 250 Nm @ 1600-3500 rpm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4541 mm L × 1752 mm W × 1507 mm H (Wheelbase: 2651 mm)
