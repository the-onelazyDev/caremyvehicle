📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

Refreshed for MY26 with Renault's new diamond emblem, redesigned front fascia, and the introduction of a punchy 100 PS / 180 Nm 1.0L Turbo petrol engine alongside the 72 PS 1.0L Energy engine, the Renault Triber delivers unmatched 100+ seating configurations with EasyFix removable 3rd-row seats, 625 litres of boot space in Life Mode, twin AC with 2nd and 3rd row vents, 6 airbags, ESP, Hill Start Assist, wireless charger, and a 17.78 cm TFT digital cluster.

Engine Options & Performance: 999 cc 1.0L Energy 3-Cylinder Petrol (72 PS @ 6250 rpm, 96 Nm @ 3500 rpm) – 5MT / 5-Speed Easy-R AMT,999 cc 1.0L Turbo 3-Cylinder Petrol (100 PS @ 5000 rpm, 180 Nm @ 2800-3600 rpm) – 5MT

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 3985 mm L x 1734 mm W x 1643 mm H (Wheelbase: 2636 mm)
