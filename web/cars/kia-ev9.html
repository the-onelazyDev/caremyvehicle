📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The All-New Kia EV9 is Kia’s flagship full-size electric SUV built on the dedicated Electric-Global Modular Platform (E-GMP) with 800V ultra-fast charging architecture. Packing a massive 99.8 kWh battery delivering 561 km ARAI certified range, twin high-powered motors producing 384 PS and 700 Nm of torque with AWD, 0-100 km/h in 5.3 seconds, 1st & 2nd row powered relaxation lounge seats with ottoman leg support and massage, Trinity panoramic cockpit with 14-speaker Meridian audio, dual sunroofs, and comprehensive Level 2+ ADAS with 27 autonomous safety features.
