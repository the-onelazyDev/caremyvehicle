📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Nissan Tekton is a rugged yet sophisticated midsize SUV inspired by Nissan's iconic Patrol heritage. Built on a rigid high-tensile architecture with best-in-class 212 mm ground clearance and 700-litre luggage capacity, it features a tri-tone beige burgundy leatherette interior with rose gold accents, dual 10.25-inch panoramic cockpit screens, E-Shifter, Level 2 ADAS, and potent Turbo T160 and 163 PS Turbo T280 engines paired with 6-speed wet-clutch DCT.
