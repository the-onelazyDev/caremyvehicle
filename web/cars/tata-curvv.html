📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Tata Curvv is India’s breakthrough mass-market SUV Coupe, melding the commanding elevated stance of a rugged SUV with the aerodynamic sloping silhouette of a luxury coupe. Awarded a full 5-Star Bharat NCAP safety rating (scoring 30.81/32 for Adult and 44.83/49 for Child safety), it debuts cutting-edge technology including capacitive flush door handles with approach auto-deploy, voice-assisted panoramic glass sunroof with ambient mood lighting, a powered tailgate with hands-free gesture activation, and dual high-definition curved cockpit displays (12.3-inch Cinematic Infotainment by HARMAN with 9-speaker JBL acoustic sound system + 10.25-inch reconfigurable digital instrument cluster). Powertrain options include the brand-new 1.2L Hyperion Direct Injection Turbo Petrol (125 PS / 225 Nm), the refined 1.2L Revotron Turbo Petrol (120 PS / 170 Nm), and the high-torque 1.5L Kryojet Diesel (118 PS / 260 Nm), paired with either a 6-speed manual or an advanced wet-clutch 7-speed dual-clutch automatic (DCA) featuring paddle shifters.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.2L Revotron Turbo (120 PS) | 1.2L Hyperion GDi Turbo (125 PS) | 1.5L Kryojet Diesel (118 PS)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4,308 mm (L) × 1,810 mm (W) × 1,630 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2,560 mm)
