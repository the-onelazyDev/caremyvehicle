📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The track-honed iteration of India’s benchmark SUV engineered for automotive enthusiasts. Featuring an exclusive WRC-inspired front bumper with red aerodynamic accents, signature N Line honeycomb grille, dual twin-tip performance exhaust with tuned sound, firmer damper settings, weighted steering rack, and 18-inch diamond-cut alloys with red brake calipers. Inside, an all-black cockpit with red stitching, metal pedals, dual dashcam, and Level 2 ADAS suite.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.5L Turbo GDi 4-Cylinder Petrol (160 PS @ 5,500 rpm / 253 Nm @ 1,500–3,500 rpm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4330 mm (L) × 1790 mm (W) × 1635 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2610 mm)
