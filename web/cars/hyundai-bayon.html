📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The all-new Hyundai BAYON is Hyundai’s latest intelligent urban crossover SUV positioned between the Venue and Creta. Featuring bold crossover proportions with signature arrow-shaped LED daytime running lights, dual LED projector lamps, wide gloss-black parametric grille, and connecting LED tail lamps with an aerodynamic rear spoiler. Inside, the cabin features dual integrated 31.24 cm (12.3-inch) curved panoramic displays accelerated by NVIDIA ccNC architecture with Generative AI voice assistant, voice-controlled smart panoramic sunroof, Bose Premium 8-speaker sound system with subwoofer, ventilated front seats, electric 8-way driver seat, factory-fitted dual dashcam, and Hyundai SmartSense Level 2 Pro ADAS with 23 autonomous safety features including Highway Driving Assist and Navigation-based Smart Cruise Control.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.5L MPi 4-Cylinder Petrol (115 PS @ 6,300 rpm / 143.8 Nm @ 4,500 rpm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4245 mm (L) × 1810 mm (W) × 1605 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2600 mm)

Language: English
