📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Citroën Basalt is India's first mainstream coupe SUV, combining a sweeping aerodynamic fastback silhouette with Citroën's signature Advanced Comfort suspension with Progressive Hydraulic Cushions, 26 cm floating touchscreen, wireless phone charging, 6 airbags standard, and spirited PureTech 110 turbo petrol engines.
