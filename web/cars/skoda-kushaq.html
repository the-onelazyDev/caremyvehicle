📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The refreshed Škoda Kushaq raises the mid-size SUV benchmark with a 5-Star Global NCAP safety rating (both Adult & Child), an illuminated front grille light band, connected LED taillights with illuminated Škoda lettering, standard electric sunroof across the range (panoramic sunroof on top trims), segment-first rear-seat massagers, an 8-speed automatic torque converter on the 1.0 TSI, and the 150 PS 1.5 TSI EVO with Active Cylinder Technology (ACT) & 7-speed DSG.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.0L TSI Turbo Petrol (115 PS @ 5000-5500 rpm, 178 Nm @ 1750-4500 rpm),1.5L TSI EVO Turbo Petrol with Active Cylinder Technology (150 PS @ 5000-6000 rpm, 250 Nm @ 1600-3500 rpm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4225 mm L × 1760 mm W × 1612 mm H (Wheelbase: 2651 mm)
