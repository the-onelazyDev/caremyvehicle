📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Mahindra Thar Roxx revolutionizes the iconic off-roader into an all-in-one luxury 5-door adventure SUV without compromising authentic trail-blazing capability. Built on an advanced stiffened hydroformed ladder-frame platform with Penta-Link rear suspension featuring Watts linkage, it was awarded an exceptional 5-Star Safety Rating by Bharat NCAP. Propelled by explosive class-leading engines: a 2.0L mStallion TGDi Turbo Petrol (up to 177 PS / 380 Nm) or a 2.2L mHawk Turbo Diesel (up to 175 PS / 370 Nm) paired with a 6-speed manual or 6-speed AISIN torque converter automatic, available in both Rear-Wheel Drive (RWD) and true 4x4 with 4XPLOR terrain management (Snow, Sand, Mud), electronic locking differential, and crawl smart assist. The upscale 5-seater cabin boasts segment-first amenities including an expansive panoramic skyroof, dual 26.03 cm (10.25-inch) HD screens with Adrenox, Level 2 ADAS with 10 active safety functions, 3-stage front seat ventilation, a 9-speaker Harman Kardon QuantumLogic sound system with 540-degree surround view camera, and a cavernous 644L boot capacity.

Engine Options & Performance: 2.0L mStallion TGDi Turbo Petrol (162/177 PS, up to 380 Nm) | 2.2L mHawk Turbo Diesel (152/175 PS, up to 370 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4428 mm (L) × 1870 mm (W) × 1923 mm (H) (Wheelbase: 2850 mm)
