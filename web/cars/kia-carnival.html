📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The All-New 4th Generation Kia Carnival Limousine redefines ultra-luxury multi-purpose transportation in India. Powered by a refined 2.2L Smartstream CRDi VGT Diesel engine producing 193 PS and 441 Nm through a smooth 8-speed automatic with Shift-by-Wire dial, it boasts 2nd-row VIP Lounge Relaxation Powered Captain Seats with ottoman leg support, ventilation and heating, dual 12.3-inch curved panoramic cockpit, dual electric sunroofs, 12-speaker Bose audio, smart electric sliding doors, and comprehensive Level 2+ ADAS with 23 autonomous safety functions.

Engine Options & Performance: 2.2L Smartstream CRDi VGT Diesel (193 PS [142 kW] @ 3800 rpm / 441 Nm [45 kgf·m] @ 1750-2750 rpm)
