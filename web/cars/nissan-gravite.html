📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The All-New Nissan Gravite is a class-defining modular 7-seater designed for Indian families, offering class-leading 625-litre flexible boot space with independent removable 3rd-row seats, dual-tone cabin with wooden finish, 20.32 cm touchscreen with wireless smartphone mirroring, independent 3-row tropicalised AC vents, and high-efficiency 1.0L petrol and factory twin-cylinder CNG powertrains returning up to 19.6 km/l.
