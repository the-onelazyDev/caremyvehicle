📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The bold MG Hector commands the road with its Argyle-inspired Aura-Hex Diamond Mesh Grille, India's largest 14-inch HD Portrait Touchscreen with i-SWIPE gesture control, Dual-Pane Panoramic Sunroof, Infinity by Harman audio, and Traffic Jam Assist Level-2 ADAS.
