📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The Hyundai Venue N Line brings World Rally Championship (WRC) motorsport pedigree to the compact SUV segment. Powered by a punchy 1.0L Kappa Turbo GDi petrol engine producing 120 PS and 172 Nm of torque, it is paired with either a slick 6-speed manual or an ultra-responsive 7-speed Dual Clutch Transmission (7DCT) equipped with steering-mounted paddle shifters. Engineered for enthusiastic drivers, the Venue N Line features a sport-tuned suspension with firmer damping for razor-sharp handling, weighted steering feedback, all-4 disc brakes with striking red calipers, and a dual twin-tip performance exhaust tuned to deliver an intoxicating acoustic soundtrack. Inside, the race-inspired cabin boasts all-black leatherette upholstery with red contrast stitching, embossed N branding, sunrise red ambient lighting, sporty brushed metal pedals, and a bespoke N-badged steering wheel and gear shifter. In its range-topping N10 trim, it elevates technology with dual 12.3-inch curved panoramic displays running the next-gen NVIDIA ccNC platform, Bose 8-speaker audio, smart aroma diffuser, front ventilated seats, 4-way power driver seat, factory dual dashcam, voice-controlled sunroof, and a comprehensive Level 2 Hyundai SmartSense ADAS suite with Smart Cruise Control Stop & Go, Forward Collision Avoidance, and 360° Surround View Monitor.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.0L Kappa Turbo GDi Petrol (3 Cylinders, 120 PS @ 6000 rpm / 172 Nm @ 1500–4000 rpm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 3995 mm (L) × 1800 mm (W) × 1665 mm (H) (with Roof Rails) (Wheelbase: 2520 mm)
