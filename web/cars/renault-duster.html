📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The legendary SUV returns to India in its all-new 2026 generation with a 5-Star Bharat NCAP safety rating, rugged 212 mm ground clearance, electric panoramic sunroof, dual OpenR Link screens (25.65 cm touchscreen + 26.03 cm digital cluster with Google built-in), Level 2 ADAS (17 autonomous features), 360° Around View Camera, ventilated front seats, Arkamys 3D audio, and three advanced powertrains: 100 PS Turbo TCe 100, 163 PS / 280 Nm 1.3L Turbo TCe 160 (6MT / 6DCT), and 160 PS 1.6L Strong Hybrid E-Tech 160.
