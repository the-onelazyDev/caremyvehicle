📝 Expert Review & Technical Overview

The all-new 2025–2026 Kia Seltos redefines the mid-size SUV benchmark with dominant styling, reinforced K3 platform engineering, and a certified 5-Star Bharat NCAP safety rating (scoring 76.7 out of 81 points). Available with three refined powertrains: the potent Smartstream 1.5L Turbo GDi petrol producing a class-leading 160 PS and 253 Nm paired with a 7-speed Dual Clutch Transmission (7DCT) or 6-speed iMT, a silky 1.5L naturally aspirated petrol with 6MT / Smartstream IVT automatic, and an efficient 1.5L CRDi VGT Diesel producing 116 PS and 250 Nm paired with 6-speed torque converter automatic or 6iMT. The progressive cabin showcases the Command-Centric Trinity Panoramic Display panel (dual seamless 10.25-inch or 12.3-inch displays), a boundless dual-pane panoramic sunroof, immersive Bose Premium 8-speaker audio, 64-color ambient mood lighting, dual-zone FATC with 5-inch digital climate touch control, front ventilated seats with 8-way power adjust and memory/relaxation position, and standard 6 airbags with all-4 disc brakes. Its segment-leading Level 2+ ADAS suite features 28 autonomous safety functions including Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist with Junction Crossing & Turning, Smart Cruise Control with Stop & Go, 360° Surround View Monitor, and Blind View Monitor.

Engine Options & Performance: 1.5L Smartstream T-GDi Turbo Petrol (160 PS / 253 Nm) | 1.5L Smartstream MPi Petrol (115 PS / 144 Nm) | 1.5L CRDi VGT Diesel (116 PS / 250 Nm)

Dimensions & Wheelbase: 4460 mm (L) × 1800 mm (W) × 1635 mm (H) (with Roof Rails) (Wheelbase: 2690 mm (Best-in-Segment Rear Legroom))
